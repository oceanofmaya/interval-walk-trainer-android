# Store Listing Copy

This document tracks the current Google Play Store listing copy and the key
field constraints used when preparing a release.

## Constraints

- **App name:** How the app appears on Google Play. Maximum 30 characters.
- **Short description:** Short app summary shown before users expand the full listing. Maximum 80 characters.
- **Long description:** Full app description. Maximum 4000 characters.

## Current Listing

### App Name

Interval Walk Trainer

### Short Description

Minimalist walking timer with intervals, goals, reminders, and stats.

### Long Description

Interval Walk Trainer is a simple, focused walking timer for interval walking workouts.

Use ready-to-go routines, including Japanese 3-3 Interval Walking Training (IWT), or create your own custom interval or circuit workout. The app keeps the timing clear so you can focus on walking, building consistency, and feeling good about your progress.

Set weekly goals for workouts, minutes, or both. Glance at weekly progress on the timer screen, with full detail in Workout History. Keep an eye on streaks, and use optional reminders to help you stay on track during the week.

FEATURES:
• Ready-to-use routines - Start with three guided interval walking formulas
• Japanese 3-3 IWT - Follow the classic 3-minute slow / 3-minute fast pattern
• Custom workouts - Set slow and fast times, rounds, and starting phase
• Saved presets - Save up to 30 favorite workouts and reuse them anytime
• Weekly goals - Track workout and minute targets for the current week
• Workout reminders - Choose reminder days and exact reminder times
• Progress history - View workouts, streaks, totals, and monthly progress
• Voice and vibration cues - Get gentle prompts when it is time to switch pace
• Voice language picker - Choose the language for workout voice notifications
• Themes and accents - Personalize the app with light, dark, and accent colors
• Pre-start countdown - Get a short countdown before each workout begins
• Background support - Keep workouts running while your phone is locked
• Clean design - Large timer, clear phase cues, and no clutter

No ads. No subscriptions. Just a calm timer for interval walking.

Start small, stay consistent, and keep walking.
